Privacy Policy — Plans With Paws Poster

Last updated: November 12, 2026

This tool is used only by the owner of the Plans With Paws TikTok account. It does not collect data from other TikTok users.

Data used: the owner's basic profile information (user.info.basic) and an access token, stored only on the owner's own computer, to upload the brand's videos (video.upload). No data is sold, shared or sent to third parties.

Deletion: revoking access in TikTok settings (Security → Manage app permissions) stops all access; the local token is then deleted.

Contact: via TikTok @planswithpaws.
